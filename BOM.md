# DownDraft BOM

The numbers are intended to be the maximum likely needed, but don't include fixings such as screws to mount the Extruder or PCB board(s).

For recommended hardware, e.g. fans, see the HARDWARE_COMPATIBILITY_LIST.md file.

## Common Parts

1 x 2510 Axial fan

6 x square M3 nuts (DIN 562)

14 x Brass heat inserts

3 x Neopixels

4 x 100mmx2.5mm Cable/Zip ties

4 x M3x6mm BHCS

7 x M3x8mm BHCS

2 x M3x12mm BHCS

2 x M3x16mm SHCS

2 x M3x25mm SHCS

2 x M3x35mm SHCS

## 5015 Parts

2 x 5015 Blower fans (6K+ rpm recommended)
2 x M3x20mm BHCS/SHCS (with fan spacer)
2 x M3x16mm BHCS/SHCS (without fan spacer)

## 4010 Parts

2 x 4010 Blower fans (12K rpm recommended)
