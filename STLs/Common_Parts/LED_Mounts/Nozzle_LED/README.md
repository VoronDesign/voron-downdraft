# Downdraft Toolhead

### Nozzle_LEDx2_Mount.stl

This part is used to retain two Neopixel LED's that illuminate the area around the hotend nozzle. The part is located using the dimples on the toolhead Front mount and held in place with protrusions on the Left and Right Fan mounts.

### Nozzle_LEDx1_Mount.stl

This part is used to retain a single Neopixel LED.

### Nozzle_LED_Strip_Mount.stl

This part is used to retain a two LED strip taken from a length of 144 LED's per metre Neopixel strip lights. The resistors on the strip should be located to the bottom of the mount to ensure correct fitment.

### Nozzle_LED_Strip_Backer_*.stl

This wedge shaped part is to help retain the LED strip mount in place.
