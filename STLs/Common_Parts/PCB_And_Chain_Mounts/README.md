# Downdraft Toolhead

### Cable_Chain_PCB_Mounts

Contains parts to mount 2 and 3 hole chains to the toolhead with heatset inserts for mounting a variety of toolhead PCB's and extruders.

### Umbilical_PCB_Mounts

Contains parts to mount an umbilical cable to the toolhead with heatset inserts for mounting a variety of toolhead PCB's and extruders.

### PCB_4mm_Spacer_x2.stl

Most of the toolhead PCB mounts require two 4mm spacers.

### PCB_10mm_Spacer_x2.stl

The supported Stealthburner PCB mounts require two 10mm spacers.
