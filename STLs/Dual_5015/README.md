# Downdraft Toolhead

These parts are specific to the dual 5015 part cooling toolhead.

The Left and Right Fan mounts house the part cooling fans.

The various Front mounts place the hotend cooling fan at different heights. The height is determined by the location of the heatsink on the hotend. See the [HARDWARE_COMPATIBILITY_LIST](../../HARDWARE_COMPATIBILITY_LIST.md) file for a list of common hotends and which Front mount to use.

### 5010_Fan_Spacer.stl

This spacer can help direct airflow from the hotend fan concentrate it on the hotend. This may be beneficial for hotends that suffer heat creep, such as the Dragon HF hotend.

### 5015_Fan_Spring_x2.stl

This spring is used for 5015 fans that can move within the Left or Right Fan mount. 5015 blower fan dimensions vary between manufacturers.

For those that stick to the specific 5015 dimensions, they may be a little loose in the mount. Use these springs to prevent the fans from moving within the mount.
