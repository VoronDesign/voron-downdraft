# DownDraft

1. DownDraft is a new general purpose toolhead from Voron Design
   
   - It uses the current CW2.* mounting pattern
   - Fits on the Trident, V2, Switchwire and Legacy Voron printers
   - It supports a wide range of hotends from the Dragon, Dragon Ace Volcano to the Goliath
   - The default extruder mount pattern is the Orbiter v2 (chosen as the Voron adjacent extruders use it), others are supported with a slim adapter
   - It supports a wide range of toolhead PCB's including 36mm and some older SB boards
   - It uses a 2510 hotend fan for improved hotend cooling
   - Toolhead height and nozzle placement maintained regardless of the hotend used

2. The toolhead offers easy maintenance
   
   - It can be built away from the printer and easily mounted with 4 screws
   - The extruder is easily removed with 2 screws
   - Easy access to the hotend when the extruder is removed for clearing clogs
   - Simple cable routing within the toolhead

3. It comes in 2 variants dual 4010 part cooling fans or dual 5015 part cooling fans
   
   - The 4010 variant is slimmer and lighter ideal for all types of filament
   - The 5015 variant is wider with more airflow for faster or high flow printing

4. It uses a modular system with common extruder and PCB board mounts
   
   - This makes switching between the 4010 and 5015 variants simple
   - Changing to different hotends is a single small part

5. It retains support of the CW2.* mounting pattern
   
   - Supports current printed and CNC Tap mounts
   - Supports compatible rail mounts

6. A wide range of toolhead PCB boards are supported with inbuilt strain relief
   
   - Universal mounts for cable chains
   - Universal mounts for 36mm boards
   - Universal mounts for two part Stealthburner boards
   - Universal mounts for the older single boards
   - Three mount points for a variety of Orbiter v2 mount pattern extruders
   - PUG support

7. Variable height hotend fan placement
   
   - For both toolhead variants there are three front parts that position the hotend fan at different heights
   - Optimises height for heatsink cooling for most hotends

8. LED support
   
   - Logo LED support for Neopixels and Barf
   - Logo LED uses the same diffuser as the Stealthburner
   - Nozzle LED support for a single Neopixel, dual Neopixel, 144 pixels/m strip

9. Extras
   
   - A mount for the Crossbow cutter by DW Tas
   - A selection of front parts with a selection of decorative cut-outs for 2 colour prints
   - LED soldering jigs
   - Custom extruder parts for:
     - E3D Roto Vitamins with an Orbiter v2 style mount
     - WWG2 front adding support for a 3rd PCB mountpoint and a choice of collet or ECAS04 PTFE retainer
     - E3D PZ PCB mount
     - Generic ADXL mount
     - XOL CNC Carriage CW2 square nut holder
     - Bowden remote extruder support with collet or ECAS04 mounts
